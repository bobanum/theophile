Content Organization

Organizing content effectively is crucial for readability and user engagement. Use headings to structure your content hierarchically, making it easier for users to navigate and understand your message.


Visual Hierarchy

Visual hierarchy is the arrangement of elements in a way that implies importance. Headings, font sizes, and colors are tools you can use to guide the user's attention through your content.
