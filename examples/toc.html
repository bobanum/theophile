Core Concepts

Important ideas and patterns when building with web components.

Shadow DOM

Shadow DOM provides style and markup encapsulation; it prevents leakage of styles into and out of the component.

Adopted Styles

Constructable stylesheets and adoptedStyleSheets provide performant style reuse across components.

Polyfills and Fallbacks

Not all browsers support constructable stylesheets; include graceful fallbacks for broader compatibility.

Custom Elements

Custom elements expose a lifecycle (connectedCallback, disconnectedCallback, attributeChangedCallback).

Properties and Attributes

Map attributes to properties carefully and reflect changes as needed to maintain consistency.
